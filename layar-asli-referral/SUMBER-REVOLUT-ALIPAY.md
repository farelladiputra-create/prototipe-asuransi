# Benchmark Referral / Invite Program: Revolut vs Alipay

Research only. Every number below is quoted from a named source. Where nothing solid was found, it says "tidak ketemu".

Date of research: 15 October 2026.

## 1. Revolut

### 1.1 The 5-step flow (what the invited friend has to do)

From help.revolut.com, "What are the requirements to get my referral reward?":
https://help.revolut.com/help/referrals/other/what-are-the-requirements-to-get-my-referral-reward/

1. **Sign up with your link** and verify their identity (KYC). Must be a brand-new customer; anyone under 18 is not eligible.
2. **Add money to their account** (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank transfer). Money sent from another Revolut account does not count.
3. **Order a physical card** (the free plan card works; Pro / <18 / Business / Joint plans do not count).
4. **Start using the card** (add it to Apple Pay / Google Pay).
5. **Make a set number of purchases** meeting a minimum spend. The exact minimum is shown on the Referrals screen. Foreign-exchange trades, transfers, gambling, and gift-card top-ups do not count.

Steps light up green in the app as they are completed. Only the person who invited gets paid; the friend gets no cash reward.

### 1.2 Menu path to the invite screen

From help.revolut.com, "What are the conditions of the referral campaign?":
https://help.revolut.com/help/referrals-new/what-are-the-conditions-of-the-referral-campaign/

Home → tap the profile picture (top-left) → **"Invite friends"**. Campaign terms sit at the bottom of that screen.

The invite screen is also pushed from other places. Screenshot 3 below shows the promo tile sitting inside the **Transfer** tab ("Earn S$45 for each friend you invite!"), so the ask follows the user into a money-movement moment rather than hiding in settings.

### 1.3 Reward mechanics (what the inviter gets)

From revolut.com referral terms: https://www.revolut.com/legal/referrals-terms/

- Three reward shapes: **Fixed** (same amount every time), **Variable** (amount drawn at random, schedules A-H listed: UK, Norway, Slovakia, Romania, Bulgaria, rest of EEA, Singapore, Brazil), **Tiered** (amount depends on which steps the friend completed).
- A separate schedule covers invites from outside the usual regions.
- Reward is paid into the inviter's account, converted into the account's base currency from the GBP/EUR/SGD figure in the terms.
- **Cap: maximum 5 successful referrals per campaign period** (help centre conditions page).
- Campaigns are time-boxed. The live example in screenshot 1 says "Ends in 14 days".

### 1.4 Growth hooks

- **Countdown pressure**: "Ends in 14 days" shown on the reward screen (screenshot 1).
- **Placement inside daily flows**: promo tile in the Transfer screen (screenshot 3), empty-state slots on the invite screen ("Your friend goes here") — screenshot 2.
- **Progress checklist**: four visible steps, ticked green as done (help centre).
- **Loyalty layer feeding the loop**: RevPoints, 17 million earners across 36 countries, up 250% (annual report 2025).
- **Word-of-mouth is the growth engine, not a side channel**: "over 63% of new retail customers joining via word-of-mouth or referrals" (Revolut 2025 results release, link below).

### 1.6 Markets

## 2. Alipay (支付宝)

### 2.1 The 5-step flow

From the official rules page (render.alipay.com, "活动规则"):
https://render.alipay.com/p/f/fd-j6lzqrgm/receive.html

1. Inviter opens the campaign screen and taps **立即赚赏金** ("earn reward now").
2. Inviter shares through a **search code (搜索码), phone-number search code, or QR code**.
3. Friend opens the code **inside the Alipay app** (a QR scanned from any other app does not qualify) and claims a red packet (红包).
4. Friend **pays a real shop with Alipay**, letting the red packet offset part of the bill.
5. Inviter receives a **random reward (赏金) straight into their Alipay balance**. Red packet types that qualify: in-store general, Huabei, Yu'ebao variants.

Key design point: the reward only lands after the friend **spends**, not after signup. The friend also keeps the red packet, so both sides get something.

### 2.2 Menu path to the invite screen

From the official "赚钱攻略" guide (render.alipay.com):
https://render.alipay.com/p/f/fd-janl6f2o/index.html

- Open the Alipay home screen, **search "赚钱红包"** in the top search box, **or** tap the **惠支付** card labelled **"赚钱红包升级"**.
- On the activity screen tap **立即赚赏金**, then choose **二维码 / 搜索码 / 手机号搜索码** and send it through WeChat, WeChat Moments, QQ, the friend list, a QR sticker, or a search code.

Corroborated by tutorial sites (jingyan.baidu.com/article/93f9803f4c985ae0e46f55c9.html; kaitao.cn; 96283.com): 首页 → 惠支付 → 发红包赚赏金 → 立即赚赏金.

### 2.3 Reward mechanics

Long-running campaign (official rules, same link as 2.1):

- Reward is **random**, paid directly into the inviter's Alipay balance.
- The friend's red packet can be an in-store general, Huabei, or Yu'ebao red packet, valid **3 days**.
- **Cap: at most 10,000,000 (10 million) reward payments per campaign cycle.**
- QR must be scanned by the Alipay app; abuse and spam reports can ban the inviter and every account under the same identity; hotline 95188.
- If several people invite the same friend, the **last code the friend used** wins the reward.

iOS App Store side-campaign, official rules (2022-09-30 → 2023-02-28):
https://render.alipay.com/p/c/aplfansday/page_u9d45e6a983ba4b76b023e0dc2066402e.html

- Friend gets a random **¥0.1–50 App Store red packet** (quota 1,000,000), must pay with Alipay in the App Store **within 3 days** for the invite to count.
- Inviter then gets a random **¥0.1–10 extra red packet** (quota 500,000).
- **Max 20 extra rewards per inviter per day.**
- Only mainland-China real-name users with a Chinese phone number and iOS Alipay 10.1.82+.
- Earlier edition (Feb 2021) ran the same shape: ¥0.1–5 extra, 50,000 quota, 20 per period cap.

### 2.4 Growth hooks

- **Double-sided payout**: friend gets a red packet, inviter gets cash-equivalent only when it is spent.
- **Cross-app sharing**: QR, search code, "吱口令" text code, WeChat Moments, QQ, printed QR stickers for the counter — built to escape the walled garden.
- **Merchant army**: shop owners paste the red packet QR at the till, so every paying customer is an invite event.
- **Bigger rewards for new users**: "支付宝新用户赏金更惊喜" shown on the invite screen (screenshot 1).
- **Anti-abuse as a feature**: reported spammers lose the reward and all linked accounts (official rules); 800 accounts were cut in the first month of the 2017 run (below).

### 2.6 Markets

The 赚钱红包 referral campaign itself is **mainland China only** (rules require real-name + Chinese phone number, no HK/Macau/Twaiwan). Alipay's global reach runs through Alipay+ partners: 300+ payment methods, 50+ wallets and bank apps, 220+ markets, 10+ national QR schemes (Ant International press material, idocs.alipay.com). Ant International reports serving 1.6 million SMEs and 30 million underserved customers with credit.

## 3. Side-by-side

| | Revolut | Alipay |
|---|---|---|
| Reward to inviter | Fixed / random / tiered cash into account | Random cash-equivalent into Alipay balance, only after friend spends |
| Reward to friend | None | Red packet (¥0.1–50 etc.) |
| Trigger | KYC + top-up + order card + N purchases | Claim red packet + pay at a merchant |
| Cap | 5 successful invites per campaign period | 10m payouts per cycle; iOS run 20/day per inviter |
| Urgency | Campaign countdown (e.g. 14 days) | Red packet expires in 3 days |
| Menu | Home → profile top-left → Invite friends | Home → search 赚钱红包 or 惠支付 card → 立即赚赏金 |
| Distribution | Share link | QR, search code, text code, WeChat/QQ, printed sticker |
| Hard growth claim | 63%+ of new customers from word-of-mouth/referrals | ¥1bn self-funded push, tens of millions of merchants on the QR |
| Published payout total | Not found | Not found |
