# Benchmark Referral / Invite Program: PayPal vs Cash App vs Nubank

Research only. Every number below is quoted from a named source. Where nothing solid was found, it says "tidak ketemu".

Date of research: 16 October 2026.

---

## 1. PayPal

### 1.1 The 5-step flow

1. **Enter invite menu** — No official in-app menu path documented ("tidak ketemu"). The program is web-first: campaign pages on paypal.com (US current: paypal.com/us/campaign/invite/terms; legacy US "$10" page paypal.com/us/invitepal still linked from PayPal's own homepage, e.g. paypal.com/fr). In-app banners/notifications may surface the offer, but no official help page documents it.
2. **Code + promised reward** — US (current): 1,000 PayPal Rewards Points per referee, both sides earn; 10 friends = up to 10,000 points. Canada: $10 CAD each, up to 10 friends = $100 CAD. Hong Kong (2022-23 campaign): HK$40 coupon per successful referral, max 10. Legacy US "$10 each" program (invitepal): $10 per side.
3. **Share method** — Personal referral link sent via the program's referral prompts; the friend must use that link when signing up. First referrer to have their link accepted wins if multiple people invite the same friend.
4. **What the friend does** — US: sign up a US personal account via the link, link a bank account / credit card / debit card, verify cell phone number, and complete an Eligible Transaction (spend ≥ $5) within 30 days of signup. Canada: spend ≥ $5 CAD in a single payment within 30 days. Friend must be a brand-new PayPal customer.
5. **Reward lands + rules** — Points redeemable at PayPal checkout and in-app options. Canada: reward may take up to 2 weeks to arrive and expires 90 days from receipt. Caps: 1 reward per referee for the program duration; max 10 aggregate rewards per referrer account per calendar year (US); 10 referrals (CA/HK). Offer may be canceled or modified at any time; non-compliance voids referrals.

### 1.2 Menu path to the invite screen

"Tidak ketemu" — no official in-app menu path documented. Entry points are the web campaign pages: paypal.com/us/invitepal and paypal.com/us/campaign/invite/terms.

### 1.3 Reward mechanics summary

- One-time, flat reward per successful referral (not tiered, not streaks).
- Reward shape varies by market and campaign: points (US current), cash (US legacy invitepal, CA), coupons (HK, via Aklamio platform).
- Both sides earn (referrer + referee).
- Caps: 10 referrals/rewards per referrer per calendar year (US); 10 (CA, HK).

### 1.4 Growth hooks

- **PayPal Rewards ecosystem**: referral points feed the same Rewards wallet redeemable at checkout, so the referral payout is sticky (user stays to redeem).
- **Honey integration**: Honey (owned by PayPal) runs its own invite ("You'll get 500 points when they complete their first rewards-eligible..." — joinhoney.com/invite).
- **Market-by-market campaigns**: same program, different reward per country (points in US, cash in CA, coupons in HK).
- No streaks, leaderboards, or tiers documented.

## 2. Cash App

### 2.1 The 5-step flow

1. **Enter invite menu** — In-app: tap the profile icon on the home screen → "Promos & invites" (official help centre). Also reachable via the share button on the same screen.
2. **Code + promised reward** — "We typically offer between $5 and $15, some rules apply" (official AI blog). Both the inviter and the invited friend are paid. Live screenshot shows "Invite Friends, Get $15" with a "Get $15" button per contact.
3. **Share method** — From the Invite Friends screen: tap "Get $ next to a contact's name" (contacts access opt-in required; ML "SUGGESTED" section surfaces up to 3 recommended friends), or type a phone number / email and tap Send Invite, or tap the share button to share the referral link.
4. **What the friend does** — Get the app, enter the referral code into their profile, link a debit card, and send $5 within 14 days of using the referral code. Family accounts (ages 13+): additionally request approval from an eligible parent/guardian and order+activate a physical Cash Card or link a debit card.
5. **Reward lands + rules** — Once steps are complete, both parties receive the bonus cash in their Cash App balance. A Sponsor (parent/guardian) cannot receive a bonus from an account they already authorized. Bonus amount varies per campaign ($5–$15 typical). Exact crediting time: "tidak ketemu" (not stated on the help page).

### 2.2 Menu path to the invite screen

Official: Home → profile icon → **"Promos & invites"** → allow contacts access → "Get $ next to a contact's name", or type phone/email → Send Invite, or share referral link. (cash.app/help/us/en-US/3135-how-to-invite-friends)

### 2.3 Reward mechanics summary

- One-time, flat cash reward per successful referral, both sides.
- Amount is campaign-driven ($5–$15 typical per the company's own AI blog).
- Hard requirements: $5 send within 14 days + debit card link (and guardian approval for teens).

### 2.4 Growth hooks

- **ML "Suggested Friends"**: top-3 recommendations on the Invite Friends screen, trained on the contact-book graph + "common friends" features; A/B tested, statistically significant lift in invitations (official AI blog, Aug 2023). Full contact list still scrollable below.
- **Cash App Fridays**: weekly giveaway campaign started 2017. Users post/retweet their $cashtag on X/Instagram for cash prizes. Super Cash App Fridays gave $10,000 (May 24 2019; June 21 2019) and $50,000. Engagement: >80k comments on the May 24 2019 tweet; +134,770 followers the next day; average +7.4% follower growth on Fridays vs +0.3% baseline (ARK Invest research). 2020 example: $35,000 Super Cash App Friday.
- **In-app Promotion hub sweepstakes ("Cash App Fridays are back")**: entries earned through real money moves; e.g. Afterpay (April 2026): shop with Afterpay Apr 3-16, 2,000 winners × $200; DoorDash (April 2026): order with Cash App Pay Apr 17-22, 4,000 winners × $100. Opt-in via in-app Promotion hub.
- **Cash App Hunt (Sept 2026)**: 5 QR codes hidden around NYC, each loaded with 5 × $20 rewards, revealed via @cashapp TikTok.
- **$Cashtag identity layer**: unique social handle for every user/merchant, the unit of the social network.

## 3. Nubank ("Indica Nubank" / "Indicar amigos")

### 3.1 The 5-step flow

1. **Enter invite menu** — Official FAQ: in the app, go to the "Cartão de Crédito" area → shortcut "Indicar amigos" → "Convidar amigos" (nubank.com.br/perguntas). Newer layout (Dec 2024 press): bottom of the home screen → "Indicar amigos" (button becomes "Indicar more amigos" after first use).
2. **Code + promised reward** — No direct cash payout to the referrer in the base program. The referrer can send a welcome gift of R$1, R$5 or R$15 (debits from the referrer's own balance) to the friend once the friend becomes a customer. Milestone reward: 10 successful referrals earns the "Brasão de bom amigo" badge. The referral also prioritizes the friend's approval analysis.
3. **Share method** — Invite via WhatsApp, e-mail, or social networks; friend receives a personal link landing on nubank.com.br/indicacao (page title: "Convide amigos para o futuro").
4. **What the friend does** — Clicks the link, enters CPF, goes through the normal analysis/approval. Referral gives the friend priority in analysis. Gamification: when the friend becomes a customer, a character is removed from the bank-ATM queue in the app.
5. **Reward lands + rules** — Referrer: badge/gamification (and historically the "Indicou, Ganhou" program rewarded per-referral amounts, now evolved into the NuCoin benefits program). Friend: receives the R$1–R15 gift from the referrer (not from Nubank). Periodic promo overlay: e.g. "Acredita e Vai" (FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022): both get a raffle number for a trip; if the friend deposits any amount or uses the credit card, both get an extra number (doubles the chance).

### 3.2 Menu path to the invite screen

Official (nubank.com.br/perguntas): App → "Cartão de Crédito" area → "Indicar amigos" → "Convidar amigos" → WhatsApp / e-mail / social networks. Newer (Mostrador, Dec 2024): home screen bottom → "Indicar amigos" → share channel.

### 3.3 Reward mechanics summary

- Base program: reward = gamification (queue, badge at 10 referrals) + approval priority + optional R$1–R$15 gift the referrer pays to the friend.
- Not a cash-back-to-referrer program today; historical programs ("Indicou, Ganhou") paid per referral, now folded into NuCoin loyalty (points for card usage, levels, optional paid membership not required).
- Promos layer temporary raffle incentives on top (World Cup 2022 campaign documented officially).

### 3.4 Growth hooks

- **"Brasão do bom amigo" badge** at 10 referrals: "10 pessoas mudaram suas vidas financeiras graças a você, isso merece ser reconhecido e celebrado com um brasão de bom amigo."
- **Bank-queue gamification**: 10 characters waiting at an ATM queue in the app; each successful referral removes one.
- **Approval priority**: referred friends get prioritized analysis;Facebook groups with 50k+ members formed around trading invites; invites resold on Mercado Livre for R$5–R$35 — Nubank states referrals must be free and only to trusted people, and suspends abusers (Valor Investe, Apr 2019).
- **"Acredita e Vai" promo (World Cup 2022)**: raffle numbers for both sides; friend action (deposit / card use) doubles chances.
- **Welcome gift mechanic (Dec 2024)**: referrer sends R$1/R$5/R$15 with a personalized message — flips the usual "platform pays" model into "user pays the friend".
- **NuCoin**: the broader loyalty program that absorbed past referral rewards (levels, perks, partner discounts).

## Cross-app comparison notes (for DANA benchmarking)

- **Reward direction**: PayPal and Cash App pay both sides (platform-funded). Nubank's base program pays the gamification to the referrer and lets the referrer gift the friend R$1–15 — the gift is user-funded.
- **Friend bar**: Cash App has the clearest hard bar ($5 send in 14 days). PayPal: $5 spend in 30 days. Nubank: approval (no transaction minimum documented).
- **Cap style**: PayPal hard-caps at 10/year. Cash App: no hard cap documented (campaign-limited amounts). Nubank: badge milestone at 10, no cap documented.
- **Viral engine**: Nubank is the word-of-mouth extreme (~80% of early growth via referrals; invite trading groups). Cash App measures CAC ≤$10 from invitations. PayPal's referral program is a steady Rewards-points loop, not the main growth engine.
- **Hooks**: Cash App = weekly Fridays + ML friend suggestions + in-app sweepstakes. Nubank = badge + queue gamification + raffle promos. PayPal = points ecosystem + Honey.
